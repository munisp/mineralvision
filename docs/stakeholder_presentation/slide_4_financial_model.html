Financial-Transfer Hardening Model

Stable Idempotency
A business idempotency key deterministically derives the TigerBeetle transfer identifier.
Replay Safety
Changed payloads reuse no transfer; lost-response retries reconcile the existing stable transfer.
Maker-Checker
The maker cannot approve; two distinct step-up-authenticated approvers are required by policy.
Audit Integrity
Durable PostgreSQL intent, approval, and event records use HMAC-SHA-256 chains; a read-only verifier detects predecessor or payload tampering.
Boundaries
The control layer deliberately has no public endpoint and rejects non-durable stores in production mode.
